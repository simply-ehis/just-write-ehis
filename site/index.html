Not a blank box. Not a project manager.

Most writing apps are either too simple — a blank box — or too complex: a project-management tool with a writing mode bolted on. Just Write ehis sits in the middle. Enough structure to keep a novel, a script or a month of notes in order; enough restraint to stay out of your head while you write.

Open it, write, close it. Everything is a plain file on your disk, indexed locally, with an editor that gets out of the way. The AI, the voice and the memory all run on your machine — or not at all, if you'd rather they didn't.

Straight
answers

No fine print. Here's the honest version.

Is it really free?

Yes. Just Write ehis is free and open-source under the AGPL v3 license. No paywall, no trial timer, no account. The browser version is free to use, and the desktop app is a free download. If you want to support it, star the repo or contribute.

What platforms are supported?

The desktop app is a Windows 10/11 (64-bit) installer today. The web app runs in any modern browser and is free to use — it stores your work in your browser. On Android, install it as a PWA and share text straight into Capture. macOS and Linux builds aren't out yet.

Does my writing leave my machine?

Not by default. Your documents are plain files on your own disk, indexed locally in SQLite, and the app works fully offline. There's no account and no phone-home. AI is strictly opt-in and bring-your-own: you point it at a local model or your own API key, and you can mark any workspace "local-only" so network calls are refused out loud.

How does the AI actually work?

You bring the endpoint. It speaks the OpenAI-compatible protocol, so it works with a local model or a hosted key. In the app you get a chat panel, a composer that streams and inserts text, a structurizer, and Tab-to-accept autocomplete. Local speech-to-text and text-to-speech run on-device, and an on-device Story Memory pulls characters and traits out of your scenes into the Story Bible.

Can I get my files out?

Always. Everything is a plain file you already own. Export any document to Markdown, text or HTML; DOCX, EPUB and PDF are built in too. Compile an entire manuscript from your Novel scenes. No lock-in, no export gate.

Web app vs desktop — what's the difference?

The web app gives you the full editor and all the workspaces for free in the browser (storage lives in your browser). The desktop app adds the on-device runtimes — local voice (STT/TTS), AI memory, and full DOCX/EPUB/PDF export — plus Windows file associations, and it saves your work as real files on disk. Same interface, more muscle.
